Joining the city
Welcome to Divergence RP

Three paths. One city.

Everything you do earns points, and points unlock new jobs, ranks, crimes and gear. Pick the side you like, or walk more than one.

Civilian
Work your way up

Odd jobs, cooking, supply runs and helping people. Climb from Citizen to running the city as Mayor.

Police
Keep the peace

Arrest the wanted and rank up from Cadet to Captain, with SWAT, Detective and the Chief's badge on the way.

Criminal
Build a reputation

Pickpocket, rob stores, run with a gang. Make it to Mastermind, then Crime Lord, and take on the PD.

Points only unlock: nothing ever caps you. Going against the police costs police points, so no one is a top cop and a crime boss at once. F4 > Progress shows what's next.

Shaking off the police
Any job outside the police earns criminal ranks. Crimes make you wanted: the police chase, and a bounty is paid when you're caught.
Scenario: store robbery

Empty the register, then get away

1
Gear up

Reach the Robber rank (15 criminal points). At least one cop has to be online.

2
Hold it up

Weapon out, hold E on the clerk. Cash flows into your loot bag while you stay inside.

3
Keep them covered

Look away too long and the clerk pulls a shotgun. Killing the clerk is a serious crime.

4
Cash out

Lose the police, then sell the cash at your fence for criminal points.

Reward4 criminal points and the cash
Cooldowneach store 15 minutes
Heatyou're wanted until you shake them
Scenario: bank heist

The big one. Bring a crew.

1
The plan

A Mastermind looks at the Banker with a weapon out and types /heist. Needs a Banker on duty and 3 police online.

2
The crew

The gang's Fugitives get 60 seconds to join. Everyone in is wanted at once.

3
The vault

Drill the vault door's cores and keep the drill cool, then plant C4. Armed guards shoot back.

4
The haul

Hold E on the cash to bag it. Downed, dead or arrested robbers are out.

5
The getaway

Sell your bag at the fence for 15 criminal points each.

Cooldown45 minutes between heists
At stakebank interest, never what players deposit
Policestop it: the Banker and Guards fight back too
Scenario: base raids

Break in, or hold the line

1
Break in

Lockpick a door, crack a keypad or set off a breach charge. That starts the raid; your gang nearby joins.

2
Read the card

The raid card (top right) shows both sides, the timer and what to do next.

3
The fight

Defenders may fight raiders without being wanted. Dead, arrested or gone: you're out.

4
The result

No defender standing: the raiders take it. Time runs out: the defenders held.

Building a baseat most 3 fading doors and 3 doors on the way in
The pathat most 4 turns, no mazes
Windowsat least a 0.75x0.75 plate, both ways
Scenario: PD raid

Take on the whole department

1
The deal

A gang Leader or Officer with the Crime Lord rank talks to Butcher by the PD's back doors and pays the deposit.

2
Get inside

Break a PD door or get a raider into the PD before time runs out, or the deposit's gone.

3
Pick a target

Kill or kidnap the Mayor, cut a prisoner out of jail, or crack the vault's three locks.

4
Hold on

Any target done wins the deposit back. Police defending the PD keep respawning: no NLR for them.
